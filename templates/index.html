Fixing "Network Connection Problem"

Browser speech recognition streams audio to Google's speech cloud. If your browser or Wi-Fi temporarily rejects that stream, apply these 4 fast solutions:

  • 1. Use localhost instead of 127.0.0.1: Chrome's security policy requires a secure origin. 👉 Click here to switch to http://localhost:5000
  • 2. Recommended Browsers: Use standard Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. (Note: Brave browser disables Google speech services by default; if using Brave, enable Google services under settings or open in Chrome).
  • 3. Windows Microphone Privacy: Open Windows Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone → ensure "Let desktop apps access your microphone" is ON.
  • 4. VPN / College Wi-Fi Firewall: If your Wi-Fi or VPN blocks speech WebSockets, disconnect VPN or test via a mobile hotspot.
