Fixing "Network Connection Problem"

Browser speech recognition streams audio to speech servers. If your browser or connection temporarily rejects the stream:

  • 1. Use localhost: Always access via http://localhost:5000.
  • 2. Recommended Browsers: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  • 3. Windows Microphone Privacy: Ensure "Let desktop apps access your microphone" is ON.
